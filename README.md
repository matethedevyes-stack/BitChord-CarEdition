Even the best apps occasionally hiccup. Here are a few quick fixes if something goes wrong:

- **App Won't Open:** Make sure you have enough free storage space. If you do, try restarting your device, then open the app again.
- **Music Won't Play:** Check your internet connection. A stable Wi-Fi or mobile data connection is needed to stream music.
- **Screen Looks Off:** If the buttons are cut off, check your device's screen orientation settings. BitChord works best in **landscape** mode. You can usually lock this in the display settings of your car stereo or tablet.
- **Forgot My Password:** If you signed in with your Google account, use the "Forgot Password" option on the sign-in screen. Remember, you can always skip signing in and still use the app.
